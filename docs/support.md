# Support

Infographic Artist is a read-only brand-system research and visual critique app for ChatGPT.

## Contact

For product support, bug reports, data corrections, or privacy questions, contact the publisher at https://github.com/ales27pm or through the publisher contact shown in the ChatGPT plugin directory.

## What to include

- The tool name that was used.
- A short description of the expected and observed behavior.
- The approximate time of the request.
- A screenshot with confidential client material removed.

Do not include API keys, passwords, unreleased client artwork, or other secrets in a support report.

## Service boundaries

The app does not provide trademark clearance, registration services, image generation, email delivery, account storage, or permanent project hosting.
